# Positioning & Messaging Prompts

- Develop a unique value proposition for [product/service] that differentiates it from [competitor].
- Create a brand positioning statement for [brand] that appeals to [target audience].
- Suggest 3 different messaging angles for [product/service] targeting [audience].
- How can I reposition [product/service] to appeal to [new market segment]?
- Develop a compelling brand story for [company] that highlights our mission and values.
