# Marketing Planning Prompts

- Create a 90-day marketing plan for launching [product/service] to [target audience].
- What marketing channels would be most effective for reaching [specific audience]?
- Develop a content calendar for the next month focusing on [theme/topic].
- Suggest KPIs to measure the success of my [specific marketing campaign].
- How should I allocate a $[amount] marketing budget across channels for maximum ROI?
