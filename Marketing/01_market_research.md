# Market Research Prompts

- Analyze my target audience for [product/service]. What are their demographics, psychographics, pain points, and buying behaviors?
- What are the top 5 trends in the [industry] market that could impact my business in the next 12 months?
- Compare my [product/service] with top 3 competitors. What are our unique advantages and disadvantages?
- Identify untapped market segments for [product/service] based on current industry data.
- What customer research methods would be most effective for understanding my [specific audience]?
